Sasha Unisex is a pioneering Russian tattoo artist who has revolutionized the tattoo industry with her distinctive watercolor-inspired style characterized by bold, opaque colors and minimal to no black outlines. A graduate of the Lviv National Academy of Arts in Ukraine with a degree in graphic design in 2010, Sasha developed her signature technique to achieve an ideal similarity between her watercolor sketches and the final tattoos, with all their delicate color transitions intact. Her work primarily features geometric animal designs and nature-inspired motifs, which she approaches as accessories that can never be lost once acquired. Initially based in Saint Petersburg, Russia, where she operated a private studio known for its warm, welcoming atmosphere, Sasha has since relocated to Europe, working from Rome, Italy, and Berlin, Germany, while continuing to build her global reputation. Beyond her tattoo artistry, Sasha has successfully expanded into entrepreneurship with her eponymous brand, producing clothing, temporary tattoos, souvenirs, and badges, while also collaborating with major international companies including Nike, BMW, LEE, Estée Lauder, and VANS. Her innovative approach has generated thousands of imitators in the tattoo world, particularly for her temporary tattoo designs, which have gained incredible popularity globally. With over 700,000 Instagram followers and recognition from publications like the Huffington Post and Mashable, Sasha's unabashedly feminine designs have found particular resonance with women, and her appointment books frequently close due to overwhelming demand, cementing her status as one of the most influential and recognizable figures in contemporary tattoo culture.
