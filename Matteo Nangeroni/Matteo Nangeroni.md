## Bio
Matteo Nangeroni is an innovative Italian tattoo artist based in Padua who has forged a distinctive path in the world of contemporary tattooing through his unique blend of surrealism, blackwork, and fine line techniques. Born in the 1990s, Nangeroni made a dramatic career shift from studying economics to pursuing tattooing in 2013, despite having no prior artistic training. His remarkable journey began with YouTube tutorials on basic drawing, followed by experimenting with various tattoo styles before developing his signature aesthetic that combines portrait techniques with surrealistic elements, creating compositions that are simultaneously beautiful and strange. In September 2018, Nangeroni founded The White Whale Tattoo Society in Padua, establishing it as an international hub that hosted over 150 guest artists in its first year alone. His studio philosophy centers on creating a family-like environment where different tattoo cultures can converge to generate innovative artistic expressions. Nangeroni's distinctive work is characterized by exquisite precision, elegant geometry, and stylish graphic compositions that often feature seemingly incompatible elements connected in thought-provoking ways. His creative process is deeply personal, drawing inspiration from his own emotions and thoughts, typically sketching at night while listening to classical music and deliberately avoiding external references. This commitment to authentic self-expression has earned him international acclaim, including the opportunity to tattoo high-profile clients like supermodel Cara Delevingne, whose full sleeve tattoo by Nangeroni garnered worldwide attention. With approximately 400,000 Instagram followers and recognition as a master of "black surrealism," Nangeroni continues to push the boundaries of tattoo artistry while maintaining his core belief that true artistic value comes from staying true to one's unique vision rather than following trends.
