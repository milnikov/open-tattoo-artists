## Bio
Pitta KM, a renowned tattoo artist based in Seoul, South Korea, has established himself as a cultural innovator by masterfully blending traditional Korean aesthetics with contemporary tattoo artistry. Born into a Buddhist family and raised with regular temple visits since infancy, Pitta's artistic vision is deeply influenced by Korean Buddhist art, which naturally became integrated into his creative worldview. His signature style incorporates Obangsaek (the five primary colors of Korean tradition), Dancheong (traditional Korean decorative coloring found on temples and palaces), and gold accents—elements that have historically represented royalty in Korean court art. What sets Pitta's work apart is his innovative fusion of these traditional Korean motifs with references to iconic Western fine art, creating pieces that bridge Eastern and Western artistic traditions. His tattoos often feature block shapes, bold animal imagery, and elements reminiscent of Japanese anime, all executed with extraordinary attention to detail and vibrant color saturation that appears almost paint-like on the skin. Despite tattooing being in a legal gray area in South Korea (where it's technically only legal when performed by medical professionals), Pitta has gained international recognition for his artistic contributions, attracting hundreds of clients annually from around the world who seek his unique aesthetic. As one of South Korea's most celebrated tattoo artists, Pitta has become a cultural ambassador through his art, preserving and reimagining traditional Korean visual elements while pushing the boundaries of contemporary tattoo design.
