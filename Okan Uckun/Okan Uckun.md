## Bio
Okan Uçkun is a pioneering tattoo artist recognized as one of the foremost innovators in minimalist and geometric tattoo styles, whose artistic journey has been shaped by his formal education in architecture and a master's degree in modern art. Originally from Istanbul, Turkey, where he defied cultural taboos surrounding tattoos, Uçkun began his tattooing career in 2009 and has since performed his art in over 50 countries before settling in New York City in 2017. After spending approximately six years honing his craft at the prestigious Bang Bang Tattoo studio, he co-founded Monolith Studio in Brooklyn in 2024 with fellow artist Oscar Akermo while continuing to run his unique art project, INM Studio. Uçkun's distinctive approach combines architectural precision with artistic vision, creating meticulously crafted designs that are tailored specifically to each client's body measurements. His process typically involves preparing three unique designs for clients to choose from, ensuring a personalized experience that transforms the human body into what he describes as a "walking gallery." Beyond tattooing, Uçkun has expanded his creative influence through collaborations with various brands and projects, including jewelry collections, fashion labels, and snowboard designs, and has served as a creative director for prominent music artists. With nearly 300,000 followers on Instagram and recognition in numerous publications, Uçkun has established himself as a transformative figure in the tattoo industry, bringing a sophisticated, minimalist aesthetic that bridges the worlds of fine art, architecture, and body art.
