Mr. K, born in Seoul, South Korea, is a highly acclaimed tattoo artist renowned for his extraordinary precision and delicate micro-style tattoos at the prestigious Bang Bang NYC studio in New York. With a formal education from Parsons School of Design, he began his artistic career as a graphic designer and created his own streetwear line called K47 before finding his true calling in tattooing. His signature style features intricate single-needle technique and remarkable attention to detail, creating astonishingly precise black and gray designs that often appear impossibly small yet perfectly executed. Mr. K's artistic philosophy stems from his lifelong search for the finest pens for his art projects, which naturally translated into his attraction to micro tattooing when he entered the industry. His exceptional talent has attracted an impressive roster of celebrity clients including David Beckham, Liam Hemsworth, Rita Ora, Odell Beckham Jr., and notably Michael J. Fox, for whom he created the actor's first-ever tattoo at age 57. With nearly 770,000 Instagram followers, Mr. K has established himself as one of the world's leading tattoo artists in the fine line and micro-realism genres, combining his background in design with his meticulous approach to create tattoos that push the boundaries of what's possible with ink on skin. His work often blends old and new styles, reflecting his personal aesthetic philosophy of mixing contemporary elements with vintage influences, resulting in timeless pieces that showcase both technical mastery and artistic innovation.
