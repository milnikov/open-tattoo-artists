Matias Noble is a world-renowned tattoo artist based in Valencia, Spain, who has established himself as one of the most influential figures in contemporary tattooing through his exceptional mastery of black and gray realism. With over a decade of professional experience, Noble has developed a signature style characterized by filigree precision in creating light and shadow compositions that achieve an incredibly lifelike quality in each piece. His artistic vision extends beyond his personal work—as the founder of Noble Art, he has built an international tattoo empire with studios across Europe and North America, including locations in Madrid, Barcelona, Zaragoza, Málaga, Zurich, New York, Miami, and Montreal. Noble's commitment to advancing the art form led him to establish the Noble Art Academy, where he shares his techniques and philosophy with aspiring artists. His exceptional talent has attracted an impressive clientele of celebrities and musicians, including Ozuna, Nicky Jam, Yandel, and Farruko, further cementing his status in the industry. Noble's work is distinguished by his ability to fuse different techniques while maintaining an extraordinary sensitivity for detail that elevates each composition. His dedication to artistic excellence and his entrepreneurial spirit have not only earned him over 600,000 followers on Instagram but have also positioned him as a pivotal figure who has helped transform tattooing from a subculture practice into a respected fine art form with global recognition.
