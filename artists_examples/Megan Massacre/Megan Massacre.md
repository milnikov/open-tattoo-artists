# Megan Massacre

## Bio
Megan Massacre, born Megan Woznicki, is a multifaceted artist who has established herself as one of the most recognizable tattoo artists in the industry since beginning her career in 2003. Originally from Pennsylvania, she developed her distinctive style through years of artistic evolution, starting with New School designs before mastering Color Realism under the mentorship of Paul Acker, and later incorporating Traditional elements during her time in New York. Her signature approach now blends these diverse influences into a unique style characterized by vibrant colors, intricate details, and a masterful combination of realistic elements with traditional tattoo motifs. Beyond her artistic talents, Megan gained widespread recognition through her appearances on reality television shows including TLC's "NY Ink," "America's Worst Tattoos," and the Australian series "Bondi Ink," which catapulted her to international fame. In 2014, she co-founded Grit N Glory, a tattoo studio and clothing boutique in Manhattan's West Village that embodies her rock-and-roll aesthetic and girl-power ethos. With over one million Instagram followers and a clientele that includes celebrities like Zayn Malik, Megan has transcended the traditional role of tattoo artist to become a successful entrepreneur, model, television personality, and published author. Despite facing early skepticism as both a female tattoo artist and model, she persevered to become an influential figure who has helped transform tattooing from a counterculture practice into a respected art form, all while maintaining her commitment to artistic integrity and personal expression.
