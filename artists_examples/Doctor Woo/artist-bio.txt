Dr. Woo, born Brian Woo, is one of the world's most sought-after tattoo artists, renowned for his revolutionary single-needle technique and intricate fine-line designs. Based in Los Angeles, he has transformed the perception of tattoos from counterculture statements to high-art fashion accessories. After apprenticing under legendary tattoo artist Mark Mahoney at the Shamrock Social Club on Sunset Boulevard, Dr. Woo developed his distinctive style characterized by delicate, hyper-detailed monochromatic designs with mathematical precision and exceptional attention to detail. His work often features geometric patterns, tiny illustrations, and photorealistic imagery executed with remarkable intricacy. In 2015, Dr. Woo opened his exclusive private studio, Hideaway at Suite X, inside the Hollywood Roosevelt Hotel, where appointments are booked months or even years in advance. His client list reads like a who's who of entertainment and fashion, including Drake, Miley Cyrus, Emilia Clarke, Cara Delevingne, Zoë Kravitz, and dozens of other high-profile celebrities. Beyond tattooing, Dr. Woo has expanded into fashion and lifestyle collaborations with brands like Sacai, Neighborhood, and Converse, bringing his artistic vision to a broader audience. A classically trained violinist and former fashion designer, his background in multiple creative disciplines informs his unique aesthetic approach. With over 1.8 million Instagram followers, Dr. Woo has leveraged social media to showcase his work and build a global brand while maintaining an air of exclusivity that has only heightened demand for his distinctive tattoos.
