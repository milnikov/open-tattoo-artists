## Bio
Yugyeong Ham, professionally known as Tattooist Banul, is a celebrated South Korean tattoo artist whose distinctive style blends realism, surrealism, and illustrative techniques into dreamy, vibrant compositions that have captivated a global audience. Her artistic journey began at the tender age of five with drawing and painting, initially focusing on anime before evolving toward colorful realism and intricate designs. Though she initially pursued fashion design at university, Banul found her true calling in tattoo artistry at age 18 after receiving her first tattoo, which inspired her to leave school at 19 and fully dedicate herself to tattooing in 2013. Currently dividing her time between Seoul, South Korea, and Los Angeles, California, where she works at the renowned nonfromseoul studio, Banul has navigated the complex legal landscape of tattooing in South Korea, where the practice exists in a legal gray area. Her work is characterized by whimsical portrayals of cute pets, surrealistic elements, botanical motifs, and a feminine aesthetic that naturally resonates with her predominantly female clientele, many of whom travel internationally to receive her art. With approximately 460,000 Instagram followers, Banul has established herself as a leading figure in the tattoo community and is deeply involved in advocacy efforts as an executive member of the Korea Tattoo Union, working toward the legalization and acceptance of tattooing in Korea. Beyond her technical skill, what truly distinguishes Banul's work is the profound emotional connection she forms with clients, particularly those seeking healing through art—whether transforming burn scars, commemorating survival after suicidal thoughts, or creating memorial pieces for lost loved ones. Her approach to tattooing transcends mere decoration, embodying a philosophy that views each piece as a deeply personal expression and a permanent part of her clients' life stories.
