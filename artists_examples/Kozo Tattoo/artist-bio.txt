Eden Kozokaro, known professionally as Kozo Tattoo, is a groundbreaking Israeli tattoo artist who has risen to international acclaim for his revolutionary micro-realistic colored tattoos. Originally from Israel, where he began tattooing at just 17 years old with a machine purchased from eBay, Kozo has since relocated to New York City where he works at Bang Bang NYC, one of the world's most prestigious tattoo studios. His distinctive style blends pop culture references with fine art influences, creating surrealist, miniature masterpieces characterized by extraordinary detail and vibrant colors. Despite skepticism about the longevity of small colored tattoos, Kozo has proven his technical mastery by consistently sharing healed results of his work. His artistic journey began with a tattoo of Salvador Dali's "The Persistence of Time," which sparked his passion for translating classical art into contemporary tattoo designs. Influenced by Old Masters like Rembrandt, Van Gogh, and Jacques-Louis David, Kozo approaches tattooing with the discipline and work ethic of traditional painters. His portfolio features an impressive array of designs that fuse anime aesthetics, watercolor techniques, and illustrative elements, all executed with surgical precision. With nearly a million Instagram followers and a client list that includes numerous celebrities, Kozo has established himself as one of the most innovative forces in modern tattooing, transforming the human body into a canvas for his uniquely detailed, miniature works of art.
